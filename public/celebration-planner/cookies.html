COOKIES & STORAGE

Cookie Policy

How Just Celebrate uses cookies and browser storage to make the service work.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Essential storage

The current planner uses essential browser storage to remember a plan on your device and to protect the enquiry journey while you confirm an email link. This storage is needed for core features and cannot be switched off without affecting them.

Analytics and advertising

We do not currently use the planner to place advertising cookies or sell browser data. If we add optional analytics, advertising or similar tracking, we will explain it clearly and ask for the appropriate choice before setting it.

Managing storage

You can clear cookies and browser storage in your browser settings. Doing so can remove a locally saved plan and may sign you out. Export a backup before clearing browser data if you want to keep your plan. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
