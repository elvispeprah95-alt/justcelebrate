How enquiries work

Just Celebrate helps you contact a supplier. You stay in control of who receives your details.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

1. Choose a supplier

Find a supplier in the directory and select Send enquiry. You can save suppliers to your Planning Portal without contacting them.

2. Tell them what you need

Enter your name, email, a short message and, if useful, your event date and location. Only include details you are happy for that selected supplier to receive.

3. Confirm your email

We send a secure confirmation link to help prevent spam and protect your enquiry. Your enquiry is not sent until you use that link.

4. We send it to that supplier

Once confirmed, Just Celebrate sends the enquiry to the supplier you selected and records it in your private inbox where that feature is available. The supplier can then reply with availability, questions or a quote.

5. You decide whether to book

An enquiry is not a booking and does not commit you to anything. Check the supplier’s price, availability, terms, insurance and suitability before paying or agreeing to a booking. Any contract and payment is directly between you and the supplier unless we clearly say otherwise.
